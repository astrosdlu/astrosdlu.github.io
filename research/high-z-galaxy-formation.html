Featured paper

A comparison of pre-existing ΛCDM predictions with the abundance of JWST galaxies at high redshift

We compare high-redshift UV luminosity functions predicted by the GALFORM semi-analytic model with observational measurements. We find that GALFORM underpredicts the abundance of bright galaxies relative to the observations. However, this discrepancy can be naturally explained by a combination of low dust content at high redshift and a top-heavy IMF in starbursts.

Featured paper

The galaxy ultraviolet luminosity function from z=7 to 15 in the COLIBRE simulations

We find that, in the new COLIBRE simulations, the abundance of bright galaxies at high redshift is lower than observed. This discrepancy cannot be explained by observational uncertainties or by low dust content at high redshift, indicating the need for additional physical mechanisms to boost the UV luminosity at these redshifts.
