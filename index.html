Welcome

About

I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate at Durham University and a member of the COLIBRE collaboration. My work connects theoretical predictions with observations by modelling how galaxies appear across wavelengths, redshift, and survey selection.

Before joining Durham, I worked at Tsinghua University and completed my PhD at the National Astronomical Observatories, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences. My earlier work focused on galaxy dynamics, stellar populations, and integral-field spectroscopy.

Research

Scientific Themes

High-Redshift Galaxies

Testing pre-existing LCDM predictions against JWST galaxy abundances and understanding the observable-space effects that shape UV luminosity functions at z > 7.

Forward Modelling

Connecting cosmological hydrodynamical simulations, semi-analytic models, and dust radiative transfer to realistic mock observations.

Galaxy Quenching

Exploring how environment, circumgalactic angular momentum, and internal structure regulate star formation in simulated and observed galaxies.

Dynamics and Stellar Populations

Using integral-field spectroscopy to connect galaxy dynamics, dark matter fractions, stellar populations, and structural evolution.
